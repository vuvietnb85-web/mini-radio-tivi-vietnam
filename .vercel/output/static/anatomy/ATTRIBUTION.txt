Anatomical meshes in this folder are not original work.

Source: BodyParts3D, The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), Japan.
Derived atlas: Z-Anatomy, by Gauthier Kervyn and contributors.
Browser packages: systems exported by DrMuratAltun/anatomi-simulatoru
(ic-organlar, iskelet, dolasim, sinir, kas, eklem), renamed here to
organs.glb, skeleton.glb, vessels.glb, nerves.glb, muscles.glb and joints.glb.
No geometry was edited. The viewer only hides, colors and merges meshes
in memory so a lesson can show one region at a time.
